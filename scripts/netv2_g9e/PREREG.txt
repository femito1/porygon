1. QUESTION
How exploitable is the deployment net? Train an agent only against a frozen cd@1350 from cd@1350's own weights; measure how far
above 50% it gets against cd@1350 (E1). A robust net closes the league / opponent-diversity route; an exploitable one gives a
training opponent that punishes cd@1350's blind spots (stage 2 = its own PREREG).

    fallback). The learner also samples at T 1.0 in training. The decisive reading E1 is at T 0.5 (both sides) as designed;
    E1 at T 1.0 (the training condition) is recorded as information.
2c. Optimizer state of runs/g9q-cd1350/final/01355036714.pt (md5 6cf40c0c): KEPT (no --reset-optimizer).

    (exp_avg, exp_avg_sq) do not depend on the lr, so the state does not "belong to the cooled lr"; keeping it is what every

6. THE SWITCH AND THE MECHANISM (no agent needed)
Switch: at the natural hand-over of the A40. netv2.env v21 is deployed 11-20 and runs/g9/MODE = g9e is written then; v21 maps
MODE g9e to g9p4 in job 1173283 (G9-P4's last job: unchanged, ends 11-21 16:41:46Z, its deadline watch stops G9-P4 at ~16:27Z,
saves and mirrors). Its queued successor 1175374 (a40, 10 CPUs, afterany) sources v21 at its start and runs G9E. Chosen over an
in-allocation INLINE switch now because it touches no live trainer (no SIGINT, no bridge) and needs no agent; the cost is ~1 day.
G9-P4 is parked first, as a POINTER park (scripts/netv2_g9e/park-p4.sh; the cluster disk is ~full, so nothing is copied): its
newest mirrored state (final_model.pt, else its newest 5M checkpoint) must load with optimizer + counters at the saved step and
no other ws-netv2-g9 job may be running; then runs/g9p4-parked/{PARKED.txt, POINTER, MD5SUMS}. v19+'s park guard HOLDs MODE g9p4
while PARKED.txt exists; resuming G9-P4 = main / user decision (move runs/g9p4-parked aside, MODE g9p4). G9-P4 is at ~510M on
11-20 17:10Z at ~1.55k sps, so its 1e9 cap is not reached first (~645M at the hand-over).
The stage machine lives in netv2.env v21 (nv2_g9e_hold, inside v17's park guard) and runs at every source of every g9 job, so
each successor picks up where the last job stopped (state in runs/g9e/ and the lineage mirrors):
  park G9-P4 -> first start (runs/g9e/G9E_STARTED) -> train to the cap -> branch point (runs/g9e/cdK/BRANCH, r-- copy of G9E's
  final at the cap) -> cooldown lineage G9EcdK -> publish its final -> readings: scripts/netv2_g9e/read.sh K, synchronously in
  the g9 job while no trainer runs (~4 x 2000 pairs, ~40-60 min on the A40's 10 CPUs + GPU; a 45-min-watchdog toucher runs)
  -> runs/g9e/rK/READING.txt (r--, written as soon as E1 is in) + INFO.txt + DONE, runs/g9e/READING.txt (r--) ->
  CONTINUE: G9E trains on; FINAL: runs/g9e/DONE => HOLD rounds.
The restart flag runs/g9/restart is re-armed at every G9E / cooldown launch, so the trainer's stop at a cap re-enters the
stage machine inside the allocation (not after the job's deadline watch or a /tmp stop; a 5th launch within 30 min in one job
= crash loop => FAILED). STATUS: runs/g9e/STATUS (every stage), runs/g9e/rK/STATUS. Failure: runs/g9e/FAILED (any stage:
park, init, branch, publish, reading, crash loop) => the g9 job HOLDs round after round until main fixes it and removes FAILED
(and runs/g9/HOLD). Note (existing watchdog rule): a successor that starts while runs/g9/HOLD exists exits at once, so a HOLD
must be resolved before the holding job's 3-day end or the A40 allocation is released.
