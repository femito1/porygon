## C3: Markov Dual-View Omniscient Critic and HL-Gauss Categorical Value Loss in PPO
- lever: training signal
- generations: both
- mechanism: Eliminate hidden-team return variance in `src/porygon/v2/train.py` by adding a **training-only Markov Dual-View Omniscient Critic** `V_omni(u¹, stopgrad(u²))` alongside **HL-Gauss categorical cross-entropy** (`M = 51` Gaussian-smoothed bins on `[0, 1]`, σ = 0.75 bin width; Farebrother et al., ICML 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03950) on both `V_omni` and the deployment public value head `V_pub(u¹)`. In `fastenv` self-play, every turn already computes both players' trunk representations `u¹_t` (encoding Player 1's true 6 sets and public belief `h₁`) and `u²_t` (encoding Player 2's true 6 sets and public belief `h₂`; cached across single-side `force_switch` steps). Because `(h₁, h₂)` contains all 12 true sets and both policies' exact inputs, `(h₁, h₂)` is strictly Markov for the self-play game. A small 2-layer antisymmetric head `V_omni(u¹, sg(u²)) = σ(f(u¹, sg(u²)) − f(sg(u²), u¹))` predicts `P(P1 wins | h₁, h₂)` and supplies TD residuals `δ_t = r_t + γ V_omni(t+1) − V_omni(t)` for GAE (`λ = 0.95`), while `V_pub(u¹)` still trains on `(u¹_t, G_t)` for `lookahead2` and search leaves.
