# Training runs and lineages

Every network training run of Porygon: hardware, job chain, start and end, steps, throughput, wall time, init,
recipe differences, parameter count, and outcome. Provenance tags as in `README.md` ([M] measured, [D] derived,
[E] estimate). Times UTC; steps are environment steps (decisions of the training side). The AUTO table is
regenerated by `python3 scripts/thesis/compute_report.py` from the train.log files on the cluster; the prose and
the hand tables are kept by hand (snapshot 2026-11-17 ~12:45Z). Results are only pointed to here; the numbers of
record are in `results.md`.

**Sources.** v2 trainers (`porygon.v2.train`) log a header line (`v2 trainer: <format> <gen>, collector …,
params=N`) at every (re)start and one `upd` line per PPO update with a timestamp and the step counter [M]. v1
(Stable-Baselines PPO, `porygon.train`) logs only `total_timesteps` / `fps` tables, so v1 wall time is the job's
elapsed time from the job records [M] (see `compute.md`). Job-dir logs live in
`/vol/bitbucket/fr1226/porygon-ws-netv2/runs/{r1,g9,g4r1bf}/<YYYYmmdd-HHMMSS>-<job>/<LINEAGE>/train.log` (v2) and
`/vol/bitbucket/fr1226/gloria/runs/<YYYYmmdd-HHMMSS>-<job>/train.log` (v1); `handover-*` and `*-parked` dirs hold
copies and are skipped. Per-segment data: `data/lineage_segments.csv`; per-lineage: `data/lineages.csv`.

### v2 (`porygon.v2`, observation spec 2.1; gen9 5,809,977 / gen4 5,702,969 parameters [M, trainer header])
Common recipe (G9q's `TRAIN_ARGS`, [M] `runs/r1/20261115-111201-1135476/train.log`): fp32 (`--no-bf16`, TF32 on),
3 epochs, batch 4096, lr 7·10⁻⁵ constant, KL anchor to the BC init (coef 0.1, decaying over 50M), critic warm-up 1M,
frozen BC-init opponents (p 0.67) + pool + 0.08 baselines, 16 self-play + 16 pool battles in the fastenv collector,
strength / entropy guards. Full method in `methods.md`.
- **R1 → R1b → R1b-F (gen4).** R1: from scratch, 11-08 (A16 710000 for 0.66M, then A30 706657 / 718701 to
  20.57M); lr 2·10⁻⁴ degraded after 7.5M (README). R1b: relaunched from R1's 7.54M checkpoint 11-09 01:20 (A30, then
  the A16 job 741849) to 20.88M, parked (`runs/r1b-parked/`). **R1b-F**: R1b resumed on a dove T4 with the fastenv
  collector and the November gen4 sets (netv2.env acda8705, 2924d92), jobs 829660 (dove04) → 1136569 (dove01) →
  1137378 (dove01, running; successor 1173311 queued), 20.93M → 430.94M. Strength-guard HOLD #1 at 390.2M ⇒ CONTINUE.
